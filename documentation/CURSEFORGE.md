# Bobber's Journal

A fishing journal and fishing improvements for World of Warcraft (Retail and Forever).

## Features
- **Catch tracking:** every cast, catch and miss with zone, sub zone, coordinates, lure, fishing skill, server hour, time spent fishing and your fishing buffs (with their source). By-catch is saved too.
- **Journal in book design** (`/bob`): fish, junk, lures, zones, vendors and teachers. Page through the list on the left; on the right each fish has an Info page, a Map page (where you catch it most) and a Sales page.
- **Sales page:** vendor and auction prices (with the date of the last scan), the value of the fish you own in bags, bank and warband, income per hour and a one-year price history chart. Prices come from Auctionator, TSM or Auctioneer if you have one installed.
- **Vendors and teachers:** what they sell, with prices and requirements, repairs and teacher services; recipes you already know are marked green. Bookmarks, and a TomTom route button if TomTom is installed.
- **Fishing journal (Retail only):** your catches and zones on Blizzard's fish pages, and your fish in the empty expansion pages.
- **Cast by double click or key** (off by default), with optional lure and weapon swap on Forever.
- Fishing tooltip with a skill bar split into skill, lure, equipment and buffs.
- Minimap button, addon compartment entry and a bobber display you can move.
- API for other addons (`BobbersJournalAPI`).
- Languages: English, German, French.

## Status
Beta. Tested in Retail. WoW Forever is not tested yet, so errors are possible there; please report them.

Source and issues: https://github.com/N3zr0k/BobbersJournal
