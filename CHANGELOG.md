# Changelog

## 0.2.2-beta.2
- Catch tracker: casts, catches, misses, zone and sub zone, coordinates, lure, fishing skill, server hour, fishing time and fishing buffs with their source; by-catch is saved with its item class
- Database per client (Retail / Forever) with zones, points and recipes, plus a public API (`BobbersJournalAPI`) for other addons
- Journal in book design: fish, junk, lures, zones, vendors and teachers with paging on the left and a map, info or sales page on the right, search and bookmarks (TomTom route for vendors if TomTom is installed)
- Sales page: vendor and auction prices (with the last scan date, needs Auctionator, TSM or Auctioneer), value of the stock in bags, bank and warband, income per hour, one-year price history chart, recipes with owned recipes marked
- Vendors and teachers: goods with prices, requirements, repair and teacher services; recipes already learned are shown green; the oversized bobber is recognized
- Fishing journal (Retail): catch and zone block on Blizzard's fish pages and our fish in the empty expansion pages
- Cast by double click or key (off by default) with optional lure and weapon swap on Forever
- Tooltip on the fishing spell, bobber display, skill bar, minimap icon and addon compartment entry
- Localization: deDE, enUS, frFR
