# Changelog

## 1.0.3-latest.1
- Skill bar: uses the size and segments of the game's own experience bar (Retail 571 x 17, Forever 1192 x 17 with 20 segments), hangs below the lowest experience or reputation bar and follows its width (Edit Mode size), scale and visibility
- Fishing log with catches, misses, zone, coordinates, lure, skill, buffs and time spent fishing
- Journal in book design: fish, junk, lures, zones, vendors and teachers, with maps, sales page, search and bookmarks (TomTom route for vendors)
- Sales page: vendor and auction prices (Auctionator, TSM or Auctioneer), value of your stock, income per hour and a one-year price chart
- Vendors and teachers: goods, prices, requirements and services; learned recipes are marked
- Fishing journal pages (Retail): your catches and fish in Blizzard's fishing journal
- Cast by double click or key (off by default), optional lure and weapon swap on Forever
- Fishing tooltip, bobber display, skill bar, minimap button and addon compartment entry
- Fix: error message "ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED" when pressing the double click key in combat
