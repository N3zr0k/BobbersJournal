# Changelog

## 1.0.9-latest.1
- Zone map: explored areas are drawn uncovered over the covered base map (Retail)

## 1.0.8-latest.1
- Fix: Deviate Fish and Darkshore Grouper were shown as junk instead of fish (added to the fish catalogue)

## 1.0.7-latest.1
- Fix: the logo of the minimap/compartment tooltip showed up on every other tooltip

## 1.0.6-latest.1
- Catch tracking: also settles a cast on LOOT_READY (auto loot, loot addons) and waits up to 2 s for the loot after the fishing ends; debug output says why a loot was not counted

## 1.0.5-latest.1
- BetterFishing installed: the casting options (double click, key, one-key fishing, automatic lures) are disabled with a note
- BetterFishing WeaponSwap installed: its weapon swap is used and our weapon swap option is disabled with a note

## 1.0.4-latest.1
- Skill bar: no segment dividers any more
- Forever: optional one-key fishing (off by default): the cast key reels in the fish while the bobber is out (untested in game)

## 1.0.3-latest.1
- Skill bar: uses the size and segments of the game's own experience bar (Retail 571 x 17, Forever 1192 x 17), hangs below the lowest experience or reputation bar and follows its width (Edit Mode size), scale and visibility
- Fishing log with catches, misses, zone, coordinates, lure, skill, buffs and time spent fishing
- Journal in book design: fish, junk, lures, zones, vendors and teachers, with maps, sales page, search and bookmarks (TomTom route for vendors)
- Sales page: vendor and auction prices (Auctionator, TSM or Auctioneer), value of your stock, income per hour and a one-year price chart
- Vendors and teachers: goods, prices, requirements and services; learned recipes are marked
- Fishing journal pages (Retail): your catches and fish in Blizzard's fishing journal
- Cast by double click or key (off by default), optional lure and weapon swap on Forever
- Fishing tooltip, bobber display, skill bar, minimap button and addon compartment entry
- Fix: error message "ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED" when pressing the double click key in combat
